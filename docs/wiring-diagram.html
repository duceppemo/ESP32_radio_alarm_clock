Notes & assumptions

  • The radio module is drawn on the shared I2C bus via the JST SH 4-pin → header cable. Confirm the SI4730-D60-GMR / PL102BA-S module's actual control interface against its datasheet before wiring — some breakout boards expose I2C, others SPI. That I2C link is control-only (tune/volume/seek registers); it carries no audio.
  • The radio-to-amp audio run is drawn as a single generic line. The SI4730 module's audio-out pins are analog line-level and feed the amp's analog input directly — confirm the module's actual audio-out pin labels against its datasheet.
  • Speaker leads are soldered directly to the amp's output; the BOM has no connector for that hop.
  • The two JST PH 3-pin cables and one of the three JST SH 4-pin cables aren't yet assigned to a specific hop above — likely candidates are the amp's audio input and power breakout. Update this diagram once wired up.
  • Panel button, encoder, and piezo buzzer wiring assumes spare GPIO with internal pull-ups; confirm against the Feather's pinout before final assembly. The BOM's piezo entry is still a placeholder (no part number picked yet) — a small passive piezo driven by the ESP32's tone() is the assumption.
  • The CR1220 coin cell seats directly in the DS3231 breakout's onboard holder — it backs up the RTC when main power is off and needs no separate wiring.
  • The FM wire antenna connects straight to the SI4730 module's antenna pad; length and routing affect reception, so keep it away from the speaker and switching power lines in the enclosure layout.
